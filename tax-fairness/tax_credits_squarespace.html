Keep Working Families’ Tax Credits

What is at stake

In 2023, Act 163 doubled two of Hawaiʻi’s most important credits for working families: the state Earned Income Tax Credit (to 40 percent of the federal credit) and the refundable food/excise tax credit, which helps offset the general excise tax on groceries and other basics.

Those expansions end after the 2027 tax year.

The timeline

  1. 2023Act 163 doubles the EITC and the food/excise credit.
  2. 2026The Legislature does not extend them (HB 2306 did not pass).
  3. December 31, 2027The expansions are repealed.
  4. 2028First tax year without them: an estimated $76 million less for working families.

What we are asking for

Extend both credits at their current levels, so families do not take a pay cut from the tax code in 2028.

Estimates: Hawaiʻi Appleseed tax model, tax year 2028; EITC claims from the Department of Taxation, tax year 2024.
