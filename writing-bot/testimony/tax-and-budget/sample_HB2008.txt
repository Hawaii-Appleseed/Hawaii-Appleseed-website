Sample Community Testimony – HB 2008 (High‑Income Tax Bracket)
Dear Chair, Vice Chair, and Members of the Committee,
My name is __________________, and I live in __________________. I am writing in strong support of HB 2008.
HB 2008 creates a new top income tax bracket for people with very high incomes. It raises the tax rate only on income above a very high level (over about $1.4 million for joint filers), while keeping the current rates for everyone below that. That means almost all regular working families in Hawaiʻi will not see any change from this bill.
Right now, the richest households in Hawaiʻi have seen their incomes grow much faster than everyone else, but they pay a smaller share of their income in state and local taxes than low‑ and middle‑income families. That doesn’t feel fair when so many people are struggling with housing costs, food, and other basic needs. HB 2008 helps fix this by asking the highest earners to pay a bit more of their very high incomes.
The extra money raised can support important public services like schools, health care, housing, and infrastructure—things that benefit our whole community and help families get ahead.
I respectfully ask you to pass HB 2008 so that Hawaiʻi’s tax system is more fair and we have the resources to take care of our people.
Mahalo for the opportunity to testify and for considering my views.
