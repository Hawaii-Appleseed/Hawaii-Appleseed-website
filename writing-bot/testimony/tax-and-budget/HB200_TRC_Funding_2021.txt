April 16, 2021

To: 	Chair Donovan M. Dela Cruz, Vice Chair Gilbert S.C. Keith-Agaran, and members of the 
	Senate Committee on Ways and Means

Chair Sylvia Luke, Vice Chair Ty J.K. Cullen, and members of the House Committee on Finance

From:	Beth Giesting, Director, Hawaiʻi Budget & Policy Center and Chair, Hawaiʻi Tax Review Commission

Re:	Request Support for Research Consultant Services for Tax Review Commission
	HB200, SD1, Program ID: TAX 107, Sequence 2000-001
	
As you know, every five years a State Tax Review Commission (TRC) is appointed to review and make recommendations on Hawaiʻi’s tax system using standards of equity, adequacy, and efficiency. I have the honor of being the chair for the Commission appointed in 2020. We have met monthly since last November and have now identified areas of special interest, including:  
	•	Adequacy of tax enforcement and compliance 
	•	Best practices to expand carbon taxes
	•	Appropriateness, value, and transparency of tax credits and exemptions
	•	Adjusting income tax standard deductions and exemptions
	•	Equity in taxing retirement income
	•	Appropriateness and adequacy of property and conveyance tax rates
	•	Strategies to assess visitor impact fees and taxes to offset environmental and social costs

This is an extensive and varied list, and, while Department of Taxation staff are very helpful, having specialized consulting resources would allow us to provide the best possible review and advice to include in our final report to you next January.

The work of the TRC is a personal and professional priority for me. I understand that investments in people and infrastructure are essential for the future of our unique home. Supporting those investments requires an adequate and reliable source of revenue through taxes. At the same time, we must ensure that the burden of paying taxes for Hawaiʻi’s public good is distributed fairly.

I want to ensure that the 2020-22 Tax Review Commission produces a report with meaning and integrity to help you in your future legislative considerations. To that end, it will be invaluable to have expert consultation available to our work.

Thank you for the opportunity to testify in support of this funding for the Department of Taxation.
