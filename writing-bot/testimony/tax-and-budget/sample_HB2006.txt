Re: Strong Support for HB2006 – Relating to Child Well-Being
Aloha Chair, Vice Chair, and Members of the Committee,
Mahalo for the opportunity to submit testimony in strong support of HB2006, which creates the Hawaii Rx Kids program to support expecting mothers and families with very young children.​
Pregnancy and the first year of infancy are some of the most stressful and expensive times in a family’s life. During this period, families face extra costs for medical care, food, housing, and baby supplies at the same time that many parents must cut back on work. HB 2006 recognizes this reality and offers assistance so parents can focus on taking care of their newborns.​
This bill provides a one-time payment of $1,500 during pregnancy and monthly payments of $500 for at least the first six months of a child’s life, with the possibility of another six months of $500 payments if funds are available. These payments are unconditional, giving parents the flexibility to pay for what their family needs most, whether that is rent, diapers, food, transportation, or other essentials.
HB 2006 focuses on families that have the greatest need by tying RxKids to Medicaid eligibility and a 300 percent federal poverty level income limit—with payments funded by Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF). This means support reaches low- and moderate-income families who are at risk of falling into economic insecurity during pregnancy and early childhood.
Research from many other countries shows that giving families direct support in a child’s first year improves child health, reduces stress for parents, and can prevent long-term problems like houselessness and poor developmental outcomes. HB 2006 moves Hawaiʻi toward this global standard, since over 140 countries provide direct child benefits.
By passing HB 2006, the Legislature can help ensure that every child in Hawaiʻi has a healthy and secure start in life, and that no parent has to choose between paying rent and buying the basics for their newborn. This is a practical investment that will pay off in better outcomes for children, families, and our local communities.
I respectfully ask you to pass HB2006. Mahalo for your consideration and for your commitment to Hawaiʻi’s keiki and families.​
