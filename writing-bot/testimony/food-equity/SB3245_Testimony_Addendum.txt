SB 3245 Testimony Addendum
Last year, a version of this bill was heard by this committee, and–amidst discussions of cuts that have now been finalized– it was agreed that “this is not the year” to put additional pressure on the DHS. The Department’s testimony stated:


They “will be implementing a new eligibility system in the fall of 2026 that will be able to support what is proposed in this measure by having the ability to implement and automate the extended 24-month certification period.”
~SB961/HCR 146 DHS testimony
 
The Legislature then passed HCR 146, which requested:


“DHS to apply for and implement the Elderly Simplified Application Project in the State when capable; and to “seek any necessary federal approvals or waivers to implement the ESAP in the State, including amending its rules, policies, and procedures”


With this in mind this committee may want to consider amending the bill to go into effect in 2027, after DHS’ new eligibility system is in place.
