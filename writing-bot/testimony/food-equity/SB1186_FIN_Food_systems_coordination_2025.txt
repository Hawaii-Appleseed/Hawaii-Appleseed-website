Testimony of the Hawaiʻi Appleseed Center for Law and Economic Justice
Support for SB 1186 – Relating to Sustainable Food Systems
House Committee on Finance
Tuesday, April 1, 2025, at 2:00PM
                                                                                                                

Dear Chair Yamashita, Vice Chair Takenouchi, and members of the Committee:

Thank you for the opportunity to submit testimony in support of SB 1186, which would establish the Statewide Interagency Food Systems Coordination Team and the Interagency Food Systems Working Group within the Department of Agriculture (DOA).
Ensuring a healthy and sustainable food system requires the work of many stakeholders, including farmers, food retailers, consumers, and community groups. Government agencies also have a critical role to play. They regulate access to land and water, administer state and federal funding, and even procure food for various programs. 
It is long past time the state recognize the critical importance of coordinating these activities across the various agencies involved. With every disaster that strikes, we witness the devastation to farmers on the one hand, and are faced with the stark realities of household food insecurity on the other. While the DOA is a critical player, they do not manage all of these roles, nor should they. However, establishing this interagency food systems coordination team will be a huge step in streamlining all fo the various government roles toward achieving the goal of a stronger and more resilient local food system.
This bill is a progressive and forward-thinking proposal that prioritizes government efficiency, public health, and a strong local food economy. Thank you for your consideration.
