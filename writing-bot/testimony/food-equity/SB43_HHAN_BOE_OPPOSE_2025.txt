	State of Hawaiʻi Board of Education
Thursday, January 16, 2025
RE: Opposition to school meal price and other fee increases
* Aloha State of Hawaiʻi Board of Education members,
The Hawaiʻi Hunger Action Network, a coalition of more than twenty local organizations dedicated to improving food security for Hawaiʻi families, would like to express opposition to the proposed increase in fees for school meals, student activities, and summer school tuition.
Many of our members represent food banks and other service providers that partner within the public school system to provide resources that school budgets cannot cover. We are deeply concerned that this proposal will have a disastrous impact on an already precarious situation for Hawaiʻi families. For many keiki, school meals are the most nutritious meals they receive all day.  
School meals are are an essential component of a well-rounded education. Benefits go beyond improved nutrition to include improved academic performance, and as such we believe they should be free, just like any other school supply. 
Instead of increasing meal prices, Hawaiʻi should be providing free school meals to students, through the Universal Free School Meals (UFSM) proposal the legislature will be considering this session (SB43). This proposal would keep money in the the pockets of the two-thirds of families who are not eligible for the free school lunch program, yet still struggle to make ends meet. 
Increasing school fees will only further burden parents struggling to put food on the table. We urge you: please don’t pass this cost onto Hawaiʻi’s hardworking parents. Mahalo for considering our testimony.
