# How to sign up, give, contact, and get involved

Practical facts for visitors, checked against the live pages on hiappleseed.org and hitaxfairness.org on 2026-10-20. Each section carries the page it came from as `<!-- url: -->`, so citing a section links the visitor to the right place. Nothing here is gated by `--public`: it is organization fact, not a policy position. Do not add a step, fee, hour, or deadline that the source page does not state.

## Sign up for the newsletter and action alerts
<!-- url: https://hiappleseed.org/subscribe -->

Hawaiʻi Appleseed has a newsletter and action alerts that tell people when their engagement matters most. The sign-up form is on the Subscribe page of hiappleseed.org, which is also in the site menu. This assistant cannot sign anyone up or take an email address; the form on that page does it. The Hawaiʻi Tax Fairness Coalition keeps its own list on its Subscribe page (hitaxfairness.org/subscribe).

## Join an issue coalition and get action alerts
<!-- url: https://hiappleseed.org/issues -->

Each issue area has its own coalition, and people sign up there for action alerts on the legislation they care about most. The five areas are affordable housing, food equity, wages and labor, transportation equity, and taxes and budget. The Issues page links to each area's page, with its priorities and research. For tax fairness in particular, there is also the Hawaiʻi Tax Fairness Coalition.

## Contact your legislator
<!-- url: https://hiappleseed.quorum.us/ -->

Hawaiʻi Appleseed's action center, hosted on Quorum, lets people find their legislators, see where they stand on the bills Hawaiʻi Appleseed is tracking, and send a message in their own words. Lawmakers listen when they hear from the people they represent.

## Hawaiʻi Tax Fairness Coalition
<!-- url: https://www.hitaxfairness.org/ -->

The Hawaiʻi Tax Fairness Coalition has its own website, hitaxfairness.org. Its Get Involved link leads to a Contact Your Legislator page (hitaxfairness.org/contact-your-legislator), and it has a Subscribe for Updates page (hitaxfairness.org/subscribe). The coalition lists the same Bishop Street office address and the email info@hitaxfairness.org.

## Give online
<!-- url: https://crm.bloomerang.co/HostedDonation?WidgetId=52224&ApiKey=pub_719d1ab5-5621-11f1-9ea1-06612d4c9559 -->

The Donate button at the top of every page on hiappleseed.org opens Hawaiʻi Appleseed's online giving form. Gifts can be one time or monthly, and monthly giving is described as the most impactful way to sustain the work. The Support page lists monthly levels of $10, $50, and $150, or another amount of the donor's choice, and describes legacy gifts through estate planning (it invites people to start a conversation). This assistant cannot take a donation or payment details. The website does not describe other ways to give, such as by check, or say whether a gift is tax-deductible; the office can answer those (see Contact the office).

## Contact the office
<!-- url: https://hiappleseed.org/contact -->

Office: 733 Bishop Street, Suite 1180, Honolulu, Hawaiʻi 96813. Phone: (808) 587-7605. Email: info@hiappleseed.org. The Contact page also has a form with name, email, subject, and message. Office hours are not listed on the website. Staff names, titles, and bios are on the Our Team page. Hawaiʻi Appleseed is not a direct service provider.

## Follow Hawaiʻi Appleseed on social media
<!-- url: https://hiappleseed.org/contact -->

The Contact page links Hawaiʻi Appleseed's Instagram (@hiappleseed), Facebook (Hawaii.Appleseed), YouTube (@hiappleseed), and Bluesky (hiappleseed.bsky.social).

## Legal help and referrals
<!-- url: https://hsba.org/HSBA_2020/Public/Legal_Assistance.aspx -->

Hawaiʻi Appleseed is not a direct service provider, and this assistant cannot give legal advice or help with anyone's personal legal situation. The Contact page directs people who need legal assistance to the list of resources maintained by the Hawaiʻi State Bar Association.

## Jobs, requests for proposals, volunteering
<!-- url: https://hiappleseed.org/opportunities -->

Hawaiʻi Appleseed posts job openings and requests for proposals (RFPs) on its Opportunities page. Openings change, so the page is the place to check what is open now. The website does not describe volunteer or internship programs, so for those the office is the one to ask (see Contact the office).

## Media and press
<!-- url: https://hiappleseed.org/media -->

Coverage of Hawaiʻi Appleseed's work is collected on the Media page (News & Media) and the In the News page. The website does not list a separate press contact; reporters can use the office phone and email (see Contact the office). The organization's Director of Communications is Will Caron, listed on the Our Team page.

## Follow bills and testify at the Legislature
<!-- url: https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/ -->

The Legislature's own website, capitol.hawaii.gov, is the official source for bill texts and hearing notices, and the place to check the Legislature's rules for testifying. The coalition alerts described above say when testimony matters on the bills Hawaiʻi Appleseed follows. The material this assistant draws on does not include step-by-step testimony instructions from Hawaiʻi Appleseed.

## Find research and reports
<!-- url: https://hiappleseed.org/research -->

Reports are on the Research page, each with a PDF, and shorter pieces are on the Blog. The Hawaiʻi Budget Primer explains how the state budget works, and Policy in Perspective reviews each legislative session. Each issue area page also lists its own publications.
