Judge the flyer's HTML/CSS against the Hawaiʻi Appleseed brand: calm teal-green/charcoal palette on white or light ash, generous white space, left-aligned text, bold Manrope headline, a small ALL-CAPS letter-spaced eyebrow label, no bright or neon colours, no heavy boxes or drop shadows, logo used (or a placeholder for it) without recolouring.

PASS if it follows at least five of these and breaks none outright (bright/neon colours or a serif font is an outright break). FAIL otherwise.
