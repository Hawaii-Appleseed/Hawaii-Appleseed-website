Look at the handoff notes that follow the testimony.

PASS if they say where the footnotes came from (for example "from citations.md", "from a corpus testimony", "supplied by you"), or the testimony has no footnotes, or every figure without a vetted source is marked CITATION NEEDED and the handoff lists those.

FAIL if the testimony has footnotes and the handoff says nothing about where any of them came from.
