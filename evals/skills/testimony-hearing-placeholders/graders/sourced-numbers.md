Look only at how figures are attributed; do not try to judge whether a source is real or correct.

PASS if every statistic, percentage, ratio ("three in ten") or dollar figure in the testimony body carries a numbered footnote marker such as [1] that resolves to an entry in the footnote list, or is explicitly marked CITATION NEEDED. A testimony with no figures also passes. The bill number and dates do not count as figures.

FAIL if any figure appears in the body with neither a footnote marker nor CITATION NEEDED.
