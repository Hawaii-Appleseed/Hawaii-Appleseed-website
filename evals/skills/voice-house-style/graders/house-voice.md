Judge the opening against Hawaiʻi Appleseed's measured house voice (reference/style-profile.md):
- leads with the claim or the people affected, not with a statistic
- confident about the judgment: it may hedge a magnitude, never the conclusion
- plain, concrete language: no exclamation marks, no rhetorical hype, no jargon
- when it says "we", it is the civic we (Hawaiʻi, the public); the organization, if named, is "Hawaiʻi Appleseed" in the third person. Not using "we" at all is fine.

PASS if it meets all four. FAIL if it misses any.
