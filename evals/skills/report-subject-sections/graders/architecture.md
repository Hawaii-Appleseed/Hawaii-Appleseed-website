PASS if the outline has the standard bookends (an executive summary or introduction at the start; recommendations and endnotes at the end) AND every middle section heading names its specific subject (for example "Who Pays the GET" rather than "Analysis").

FAIL if any middle heading is a generic label such as Background, Analysis, Findings, Discussion or Methodology, or if the recommendations or endnotes are missing.
