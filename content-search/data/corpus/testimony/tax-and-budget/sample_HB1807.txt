Sample Community Testimony – HB 1807 (High‑Earners Surtax)
Dear Chair, Vice Chair, and Members of the Committee,
My name is __________________, and I am a resident of __________________. I am writing in strong support of HB 1807.
HB 1807 adds a small extra tax only on very high incomes. It would place a 3% surtax on income above certain levels: over $1,000,000 for couples filing jointly, over $750,000 for heads of household, and over $500,000 for single filers. That means regular working families like mine would not pay this tax at all. It only applies to the very highest earners.
Right now, low‑income families in Hawaiʻi pay a bigger share of their income in state and local taxes than the richest households. At the same time, we are struggling to fund schools, colleges, transportation, and other basic services. HB 1807 helps fix this by asking those who are doing extremely well to contribute a little more.
The money from this surtax would go to public education and transportation. That means better schools, safer roads, and stronger communities for all of us.
I respectfully ask you to pass HB 1807 so that Hawaiʻi can raise needed revenue in a fair way and reduce the burden on families who are already struggling with high costs.
Mahalo for the opportunity to testify and for considering my views.
