Calendar from selection

Privacy notice and terms of use for the internal Google Docs add-on used by Hawaiʻi Appleseed staff.

This add-on is private to the Hawaiʻi Appleseed Workspace domain. It is not offered to the public and is not listed in the public Google Workspace Marketplace.

Privacy notice

What the add-on can access. When you open the side panel in a Google Doc, the add-on reads the text of that document only, so it can find dates and times in what you have selected or in the document body. It uses your Google Calendar to create an event when you ask it to, and Drive access limited to files the add-on itself creates or that you explicitly open with it.

What it does with that text. It parses it for a date and time, in memory, to fill in the panel. That is all.

What it stores. Nothing. There is no database, no server, and no logging of document content. The add-on runs as Google Apps Script inside your own Google account; document text is not copied anywhere outside Google's services.

What it shares. Nothing with anyone. No third parties, no analytics, no advertising, no data sale or transfer. Events you create are created on your own calendar, under your own account.

Who can use it. Only accounts in the Hawaiʻi Appleseed Workspace domain.

Terms of use

  • This is an internal tool provided to Hawaiʻi Appleseed staff for organizational work, at no charge.
  • It is provided as-is, without warranty. Always check a date it proposes before relying on it — the parser is a convenience, not an authority.
  • Use it in accordance with your organization's normal policies for handling Hawaiʻi Appleseed documents and calendars.
  • Access may be changed or withdrawn by an administrator at any time.
